Use

Three ways to make speech

1 · Clone a voice

Give a clean 4–9 s clip and exactly what is said in it. VoxPocket reads new sentences in that voice.

2 · Clone + style

Put a style in brackets before the text: (sad, low and slow tone)សួស្តី… Happy, sad, angry, whisper, surprised, news reader…

3 · Design a voice

No clip at all: describe the voice. (A young woman, clear and bright voice)សួស្តី…

from voxcpm.core import VoxCPM
import soundfile as sf

m = VoxCPM.from_pretrained(hf_model_id="INGMUYLEANG/VoxPocket-v5", load_denoiser=False)
sr = m.tts_model.sample_rate  # 44100

# 1) clone a voice: a clean 4–9 s clip + exactly what is said in it
wav = m.generate(text="សួស្តីបងប្អូនទាំងអស់គ្នា។", prompt_wav_path="my_voice.wav",
                 prompt_text="<words spoken in my_voice.wav>", cfg_value=2.0, inference_timesteps=10)
# 2) clone + style
wav = m.generate(text="(angry and upset tone)ហេតុអ្វីបានជាអ្នកមកយឺតម្ដងទៀត?", prompt_wav_path="my_voice.wav",
                 prompt_text="<words spoken in my_voice.wav>")
# 3) design a voice, no clip
wav = m.generate(text="(A young woman, clear and bright voice, cheerful tone)សួស្តី។")
sf.write("voxpocket.wav", wav, sr)

Tips: short clean reference clips with their exact words; split long text into sentences.

Docs

How VoxPocket works

VoxPocket is VoxCPM 1.5 (an open, tokenizer-free TTS model) fine-tuned on Khmer over several rounds, with speech distilled from the bigger VoxCPM2.

The data · ~1,010 h

237 h of real Khmer speech (double weight, so voices stay natural), ~600 h of synthetic VoxCPM2 speech checked by a speech recognizer (kept only ≤ 15% errors), and 157 h of voice clip + style pairs. 340 h are new in v5: emotions, long reading, English words inside Khmer.

The model · 0.89B

A text-semantic language model (24 layers) plans the speech; a residual acoustic model and a local diffusion transformer add the voice and tone; the AudioVAE decoder makes the 44.1 kHz waveform. No audio tokenizer.

The training

From the previous VoxPocket, one more full pass over the data (778,028 clips): learning rate 2e-5 with warm-up, 43,200 steps, about 14 h on one RTX 5090. Every release is checked by listening back with a Khmer speech recognizer.

Limits. Very long text in one go still makes mistakes: split it into sentences. Styles change cloned voices less than designed voices. Rare names can be swapped; English is basic. Excited, Angry and Surprised are used with Khmer text for now; for English-only text they are coming soon (the demo reads those lines in a plain tone). All styles work with Khmer and Khmer + English text. Do not impersonate real people; clone only voices you have permission to use.
Dataset

VoxPocket Khmer TTS dataset

Synthetic Khmer speech made with VoxCPM2, every clip read back by a speech recognizer and kept only when it was right.

Full dataset Request access

102,224 clips · 94.9 h · 44.1 kHz FLAC. Khmer, Khmer + English and English; cloned, styled and designed voices; text, style, voice and quality columns. Access is approved by hand.
